## Positioning (draft, based on what we've built so far)

9gear Pulse is a **trust-first AI data engineering tool** — its whole
value proposition depends on being credible enough that a stranger
hands it their database credentials. That should shape brand tone more
than typical dev-tool "move fast" energy: precise, calm, transparent
about what the AI is and isn't doing, rather than hype-driven.

**Suggested tagline direction** (draft, not final):
*"Describe the pipeline. Review what it builds. Ship it."* — emphasizes
the human-in-the-loop review step, which is also your key security/trust
differentiator versus a fully autonomous competitor.

## Voice & Tone Guidelines (draft)

- **Precise over hype.** Say what the product does, not what it might
  someday do. Credibility compounds with a technical audience; overclaiming
  erodes it fast.
- **Show the work.** Wherever possible, surface the generated code, the
  diff, the schema metadata — transparency is the brand, not just a
  feature.
- **Calm under pressure.** Error states and failed runs should read as
  informative, not alarming — this is a tool people will trust with
  production data.
